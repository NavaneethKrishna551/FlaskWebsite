Ultrasonic sensor distance detection with arduino



A simple distance detection system that activates three colored leds at different lengths

I made it a long time ago and later attached an LCD display to it to show the exact distance

Pro(s)

Very simple and scalable.

Con(s)

Different reading based on material.

Items used
  1. Arduino UNO x1
  2. LCD screen module x1
  3. HC-SR04 Ultrasonic sensor x1
  4. Colored LEDs x3
  5. Jumpers and other wires
